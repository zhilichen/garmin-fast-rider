FastRider

Full-screen cycling data for Garmin Edge, with clear zone colors and lap power trends.

See the numbers that matter at a glance:
• Large heart rate and 3s/10s average power, with independent zone colors.
• A moving mark on each color bar shows your position within the current zone.
• Rear gear, cadence, estimated gradient and current speed.
• All-ride or current-lap duration, average power and distance.
• A power graph with 5-minute, 20-minute and All ranges, FTP and Z2 guides, and native lap markers.
• Total ascent and the current AM/PM clock above the graph.

Touch-enabled Edge devices:
Tap power to switch 3s/10s; tap statistics to switch All/Lap; tap the graph to change its range. A native manual or automatic lap selects the latest lap. Double-tap the left heart-rate/live-data area to play a two-second electronic bell effect, where supported by the device.

Button-only Edge 530, 540 and 550:
Use the default 3s / All / 5m view. Touch controls and the electronic bell are not included.

Setup:
Add FastRider to a cycling activity data screen and choose a one-field, full-screen layout. Defaults are 3s / All / 5m. Units in this version are mi, mph and ft.

Data and sensors:
Heart rate, power and cadence require the corresponding data source. Rear gear requires compatible electronic shifting. Missing values appear as dashes. Cycling FTP and heart-rate zones use your Garmin profile when available; fallback values can be changed in app settings.

Gradient is estimated from measured altitude and distance. Remaining distance is shown only when Garmin supplies valid distance to a named navigation destination. Future course elevation is not displayed. The graph contains samples collected since the field started; it cannot restore earlier ride history. Lap average power is locally estimated and may differ from Garmin's native lap statistic.

Free and open source. No advertising, external account or developer data collection. Screenshots use synthetic sample metrics.
