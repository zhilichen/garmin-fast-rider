FastRider – Ride in Color

Power, heart rate, lap stats and your ride essentials — on one screen!

FastRider turns your Garmin Edge into a clear, colorful cycling dashboard. Big numbers, useful training guides and a friendly touch bell keep the essentials close, whether you ride on the road, explore new routes or train indoors.

YOUR RIDE, COLOR CODED
Power and heart rate use bold zone colors, with a moving mark showing where you are inside the current zone. Cadence and gradient have their own color alerts. Power uses the same colors in the live number and graph: blue for Z2, orange for Z4, purple for Z5, red for Z6/Z7, and gray for Z1/Z3. Spot a change in effort at a glance.

A POWER GRAPH FOR Z2 + THRESHOLD
Ride close to the Z2 ceiling while staying blue. For long threshold intervals, including 3 × 20 minutes, hold orange through Z4. The graph includes a Z2 ceiling guide, an FTP guide and native lap markers. Choose the last 5 minutes, 20 minutes or the whole ride. Colors help show when you leave your target zone; FastRider does not run or prescribe a workout.

DOUBLE-TAP. DING!
No built-in bike-bell feature? On touch-enabled models, double-tap the left heart-rate/live-data area for a two-second, three-strike electronic bell effect. It uses the Edge's supported tone playback — no companion app or separate bell feature required. Sound and loudness depend on device hardware; this is not the native Garmin bike-bell function.

ALL THE ESSENTIALS
• Large heart rate and 3s/10s average power.
• Rear gear, cadence, estimated gradient and speed.
• All-ride/current-lap time, average power and distance.
• Total ascent, AM/PM clock and power history.
• Remaining distance when Garmin supplies valid navigation-to-destination data.

TAP TO MAKE IT YOURS
Tap power: 3s / 10s. Tap statistics: All / current lap. Tap the graph: 5m / 20m / All. Native manual or automatic lap boundaries select the latest lap. Button-only Edge 530, 540 and 550 use the fixed 3s / All / 5m view and do not include touch controls or the electronic bell.

SETUP + DATA
Add FastRider to a cycling activity page and select a one-field, full-screen layout. Units are mi, mph and ft. Compatible heart-rate, power, cadence and electronic-shifting data sources are needed for those values; missing values show dashes. FTP and heart-rate zones use your cycling profile when available, with app-setting fallbacks.

Gradient is estimated from altitude and distance. History starts when the field runs; earlier ride samples and future course elevation are unavailable. Lap average power is locally estimated and may differ from Garmin's own lap statistic. Screenshots use synthetic example data, including illustrative training sessions.

Free and open source. No ads, external account or developer data collection. Independent project; not affiliated with Garmin.
