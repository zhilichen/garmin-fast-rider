First public release — v0.4.1

• One full-screen dashboard for 12 Garmin Edge models.
• Zone-colored heart rate and 3s/10s power, with within-zone position markers.
• Gear, cadence, speed, gradient and native-lap/whole-ride statistics.
• A 5m / 20m / All power graph with Z2 and FTP guides and lap markers.
• A two-second electronic bell effect and tap controls on touch models.
• Larger adaptive numbers for tall and high-resolution displays; the approved Edge 840 layout is preserved.
• Default 3s / All / 5m view for button-only Edge 530, 540 and 550.

Units: mi / mph / ft. Screenshots show synthetic example data.
