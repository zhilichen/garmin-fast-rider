Updates from v0.4.3–v0.4.13

• New automatic paused summary: pause your ride to see current/average heart rate, average power, NP, distance, ascent, average cadence and average speed. Resume to return to your riding dashboard.

• Clearer heart-rate and power zones: larger numbered badges and tapered gauges show your position within each zone.

• Bigger riding numbers: gear, cadence, gradient, speed and distance are easier to read.

• Improved All/Lap statistics: compact time formatting and larger distance readings, with remaining navigation miles displayed in blue when available.

• Cleaner paused layout: short labels, units beside values, larger statistics and a top-right clock.

• Better fitting for long readings, including four-digit power and large distances.

• Improved memory handling on older Edge devices, plus refreshed promotional screenshots.

NP is calculated locally. Partial recordings or missing power samples are marked “NP est” and may differ from Garmin’s post-ride value.
